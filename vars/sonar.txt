<p>
Analyzes project with sonar and publishes to https://sonar.ivteam.io

A maven environment is required on the executing agent.

</p>
<p>
Supported Parameters:
</p>
<dl>
    <dt><code>projectName</code></dt>
    <dd>
        <i>Mandatory</i> project name in sonarqube.
    </dd>
</dl>
<dl>
    <dt><code>mvnPhase</code></dt>
    <dd>
        <i>Optional</i> maven lifecycle phase. default: package.
    </dd>
</dl>
<dl>
    <dt><code>mvnArgs</code></dt>
    <dd>
        <i>Optional</i> additional maven arguments. default: empty.
    </dd>
</dl>

<p>
    <b>Example:</b>
</p>
<pre>
    sonar(projectName: 'engine-cockpit', mvnPhase: 'clean verify', mvnArgs: '-Dsonar.exclusions=**/.p12')
</pre>
